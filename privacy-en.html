Motiv is a productivity and digital wellbeing app developed independently by Angelica Lima. This policy explains, simply and directly, how Motiv handles your data.

Summary

Motiv does not collect, track, or share any personal data about you. All information used by the app — your blocking schedules, your goals, your streak history, and the health/activity data you authorize — is stored only on your own iPhone, using the system's local storage (UserDefaults) and Apple's native permissions. Nothing is sent to external servers, ours or third parties'.

What data Motiv accesses

Apple HealthKit (optional)

If you authorize it, Motiv reads physical activity data (such as steps or workouts) directly from Apple Health to calculate your progress toward your daily goals. This read happens locally through Apple's operating system; Motiv never sends this data outside the device.

Screen Time / Family Controls (Apple)

Motiv uses Apple's official APIs (Family Controls and Device Activity) to block or allow access to other apps according to the rules you set yourself. These permissions are managed entirely by Apple's operating system; Motiv does not receive, view, or store the content of what you do in other apps — it only applies the blocking you defined.

In-app preferences and progress

Your settings, goals, streak history, and your virtual pet's (Mochi) progress are saved locally on your device, using iOS's standard storage. This information never leaves your iPhone.

What Motiv does NOT do

  • Does not collect personally identifiable data (name, email, location, contacts, etc.)
  • Does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs
  • Does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties
  • Does not track you across apps or websites (Apple's "App Tracking Transparency" does not apply because Motiv simply doesn't track)

These practices are formally declared in the Privacy Manifest that ships with the app on the App Store, as required by Apple.

Purchases and subscription

Motiv's purchases and subscriptions are processed entirely through Apple's StoreKit. Motiv has no access to your card or Apple account data — that is managed exclusively by Apple, under Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

Motiv is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from minors.

Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated, the new update date will be shown at the top of this page. We recommend reviewing it periodically.

Contact
